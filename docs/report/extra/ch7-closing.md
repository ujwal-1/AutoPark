## Concluding remarks

A parking bay is free if and only if a vehicle has entered and has not yet left. That
sentence is the whole design. It replaces a sensor in every space with two observations
at the boundary, and it turns the difficult part of the problem from instrumentation into
decision-making under uncertainty — which is where the recognition pipeline, the refusal
policy and the audit trail earn their place. The prototype demonstrates that the
substitution is workable at the scale of a department lot, and that its costs are
predictable: recognition quality, one recovery path for unreadable exits, and a policy
decision about unbooked arrivals. Those costs are worth stating plainly, because they are
the terms on which gate-only occupancy becomes a credible alternative to instrumenting
every bay.
